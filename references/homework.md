# Homework inside the lesson

Use when assignments or supplied exercises are in scope. A learner should reach the question, recognize the required knowledge, and follow how it becomes a solution inside the guide.

## Capture the actual task

Include each requested question's wording, subparts, values, constraints, diagrams/tables, and instructions, with filename/page/question locators. A translation accompanies rather than replaces the source wording. Compare extracted text with the image. Missing/unreadable questions remain explicit gaps; do not invent an alleged original from its topic.

Private integration does not authorize public publication of a learner's course materials. Public demonstrations use original authored or appropriately licensed questions.

## Connect question and lesson

Map each subpart: what it asks → knowns/unknowns → prerequisite concepts → source pages and guide sections → method and conditions. Place a single-module question after its explanation. Put a cross-module question after the last necessary module, with backward links. Maintain an assignment index so no subpart disappears.

## Teach the solution

Present the full question before solving it. Explain what it asks and why the method fits, then show:

1. Givens, unknowns, conditions, units or evidence requirements.
2. Relevant relation/reasoning structure and the module that taught it.
3. Setup: choose the model, function, argument, algorithm, or procedure and justify it.
4. Each transformation/inference, its reason, and new notation beside it.
5. Calculation, code trace, evidence evaluation, or interpretation.
6. Check every subpart against the question, including units/domain, support, rounding, and limitations.
7. A recognition cue for deciding when this method applies in a future question.

An answer key alone is insufficient. Explain a plausible mistaken method when it addresses the learner's actual confusion. Separate official answers from tutor solutions; if they conflict, preserve both, verify assumptions/calculation, and identify the conflict. Discussion questions get supported reasoning and possible alternatives, not invented scoring rules.

For ordinary study requests give worked solutions by default; follow the learner's hints-first/attempt-review mode and supplied assignment rules. Label self-authored practice and its new values. Never substitute its data for original homework conditions.

## Completion

Every requested question/subpart is accurately reproduced, mapped to concepts, and resolved to the permitted depth or marked blocked. The lesson teaches the foundations the solution uses, so each step can be traced back to where it was learned.
